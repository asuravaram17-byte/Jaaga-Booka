JAAGA BOKKA

class 9th lo, kooni situations valla memu first benches lo kurchoka thappaledu.Apuudey unko gumpu ni kalisam, kalavalsivachindhi.Bhavya(metta),Mohitha(MOTHA),Eshita(yet to be decided).Vellani kalisina tharavatha, em ayindho telidu kaani,antha maripoyindhi, anukokunda friends ayipoyam, anthakaana ekkuva ayipoyam, andharam M*DDA G*DISIPOYAM.Anukokunda oka gumpu la maripoyam(jaaga bokka).

Tharuvaatha antha same,JAAGA BOKKA anedhi oka emotion, khahdu oka motion .People in motion, a grou of people(erripukulu)in motion.Enudku kalusthamo telidhu, kalisi em chesthamo telidhu, kaani kalustham, kamma dengkuntam thinatam, pantam etc.Kaani evvadu "entra ee pukku gola" ani anukoledu, ankunna, baitaki chepuukoledu, cheppiina evvadu pattinchukoledu.
